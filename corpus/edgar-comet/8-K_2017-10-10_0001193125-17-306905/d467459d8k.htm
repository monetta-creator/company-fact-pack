Section 8 – Other Events.

 

Item 8.01. Other Events.

On October 10, 2017 (the “Closing Date”), the Capital One Multi-asset Execution Trust issued its Class A(2017-4) Notes, Class A(2017-5) Notes and Class A(2017-6) Notes.

Credit Risk Retention

On the Closing Date, the Master Trust Transferor Interest was $3,069,933,226, representing approximately 13.72% of the aggregate adjusted outstanding investor ABS interests as of the Closing Date, measured in accordance with the provisions of the U.S. risk retention rules.
