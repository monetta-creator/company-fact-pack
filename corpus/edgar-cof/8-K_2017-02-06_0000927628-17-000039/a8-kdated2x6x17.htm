Item 5.02    Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

(e) On February 2, 2017, the Compensation Committee (the “Committee”) and the independent members of the Board of Directors (the “Independent Directors”) of Capital One Financial Corporation (the “Company”) approved the 2017 compensation plans for Mr. Richard D. Fairbank, the Company’s Chair, Chief Executive Officer and President, and the Company’s other named executive officers (the “Named Executive Officers”). Consistent with the Company’s long-standing practice, the compensation plans take effect immediately and are designed to directly link Mr. Fairbank’s and the Named Executive Officers’ compensation with the Company’s performance over multiple time horizons and to align their interests with the interests of the Company’s stockholders. In addition, the Committee and the Independent Directors approved incentive awards to Mr. Fairbank and the Named Executive Officers for the 2016 performance year.
2016 Incentive Award for the Chief Executive Officer
In February 2016, the Committee and the Independent Directors approved a 2016 compensation plan for Mr. Fairbank that included the opportunity to receive an award consisting of deferred cash, a cash-settled award or both in late 2016 or early 2017 based on the Company’s actual performance in 2016. After evaluating the Company’s performance in 2016, on February 2, 2017, the Committee and the Independent Directors approved awards to Mr. Fairbank totaling $4.46 million. The award consists of a deferred cash bonus in the amount of $2.68 million and a grant of 20,675 restricted stock units (“RSUs”). The deferred cash bonus is mandatorily deferred for three years into the Company’s Voluntary Non-Qualified Deferred Compensation Plan and will pay out in the first calendar quarter of 2020. The award of RSUs will vest in full on February 15, 2020, and settle in cash based on the Company’s average stock price over the fifteen trading days preceding the vesting date. Both the deferred cash bonus and the cash-settled RSUs are subject to the same clawback and, with respect to the cash-settled RSUs, performance-based vesting provisions as described in the Proxy Statement for the 2016 Annual Stockholder Meeting (the “2016 Proxy Statement”) with respect to the deferred cash bonus and cash-settled RSUs granted to Mr. Fairbank in February 2016.
2017 Compensation Plan for the Chief Executive Officer
On February 2, 2017, the Committee and the Independent Directors approved a total target compensation amount of $17.5 million for Mr. Fairbank’s 2017 compensation plan. The plan is identical in amount and substantially the same in structure to the 2016 compensation plan for Mr. Fairbank and consists of equity grants that are completely at-risk based on the Company’s performance, with payout opportunities deferred for three years, as well as an opportunity for an incentive award in late 2017 or early 2018 based on the Company’s actual performance in 2017. Under the plan, on February 2, 2017, the Committee and the Independent Directors granted Mr. Fairbank a performance share award providing the opportunity to receive from 0 to 150% of the target number of 101,344 shares of the Company’s common stock based on the Company’s performance over the three-year period beginning on January 1, 2017. The Company’s performance will be assessed on the basis of the Company’s Adjusted ROA relative to a peer group consisting of companies in the KBW Bank Sector index, excluding custody banks. After the end of the three-year performance period, the Committee will certify the Company’s performance and issue the corresponding number of shares of the Company’s common stock, if any. The number of shares that will be issued after the Committee certifies the Company’s performance will be reduced in the event that the Company’s Adjusted ROA for one or more fiscal years during the performance period is not positive, regardless of how well the Company’s Adjusted ROA compares to the peer group. If the Company’s Adjusted ROA is not positive for all three fiscal years in the performance period, Mr. Fairbank will forfeit the entire award of performance shares. The performance shares are subject to clawback provisions as described in the 2016 Proxy Statement with respect to the performance shares granted to Mr. Fairbank in February 2016, including clawback in the event of a financial restatement by the Company.
Mr. Fairbank also received a grant of 81,486 nonstatutory stock options at an exercise price of $86.34 per share (which was the fair market value of the Company’s common stock on the date of grant). The options will become fully exercisable on February 15, 2020, and expire ten years from the date of grant. Mr. Fairbank can only realize value from the stock options if and to the extent the Company’s stock price increases after the date of grant and the market value of the stock exceeds the exercise price at the time the options are exercised. The stock options are subject to the same performance-based vesting and clawback provisions as described in the 2016 Proxy Statement with respect to the stock options granted to Mr. Fairbank in February 2016.
Mr. Fairbank also has an opportunity to receive an award in late 2017 or early 2018 based on the Company’s actual performance in 2017 solely in the discretion of the Committee and the Independent Directors. Any such award will consist of deferred cash, a cash-settled award or both. The Committee’s and the Independent Directors’ determination regarding whether to make the award, the form of the award and the value of the award relative to the target amount of $7,000,000, will be based

2016 Incentive Awards for the Named Executive Officers
On February 2, 2017, the Committee and the Independent Directors granted to the Named Executive Officers awards including stock-settled RSUs, cash-settled RSUs, performance shares and/or stock options in recognition of the Company’s and the executives’ performance in 2016. The terms and conditions of these incentive awards are comparable to the awards granted to the Named Executive Officers for 2015, as described in the 2016 Proxy Statement. The stock-settled RSUs and the stock options are subject to the same performance-based vesting provisions as described in the 2016 Proxy Statement with respect to the stock-settled RSUs and stock options granted to the Named Executive Officers in February 2016, and all of the awards are subject to the same clawback provisions as described in the 2016 Proxy Statement with respect to the incentive awards granted to the Named Executive Officers in February 2016.
2017 Compensation Plan for the Named Executive Officers
The 2017 compensation plan approved for the Named Executive Officers is substantially the same as the 2016 compensation plan. The Named Executive Officers’ total target compensation ranges between $4.0 million and $8.2 million. Approximately 20% of each Named Executive Officer’s total target compensation will be paid as regular cash salary throughout the performance year and 15% of total target compensation will be paid in the form of restricted stock units granted on February 2, 2017. The restricted stock units will vest on January 1, 2018, and settle in cash on February 15, 2018 based on the Company’s average stock price for the fifteen trading days preceding the payment date.
Approximately 15% of each Named Executive Officer’s total target compensation is expected to be delivered in the form of cash-settled awards. The awards, if any, will be determined in late 2017 or early 2018 solely in the discretion of the Committee and the Independent Directors based on a variety of company performance factors assessing the Company’s actual performance in 2017.
The remaining 50% of the Named Executive Officers’ total target compensation is expected to consist of equity incentive awards in the form of restricted stock units that settle in shares of the Company’s common stock and other equity-based awards. These equity awards, if any, will be granted to the Named Executive Officers in late 2017 or early 2018 solely in the discretion of the Committee and the Independent Directors and will be completely at-risk based on the individual executive’s performance in 2017.
