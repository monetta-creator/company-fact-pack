The following is a message sent to the officers and directors of Discover Financial Services by J. Michael Shepherd on May 24, 2024.

 

 

To: Officers & Directors

From: Michael Shepherd

Subject: May 14 Special Town Hall Follow-up for Officers & Directors

Dear Officer & Directors,

Thank you for attending our Special Town Hall last Tuesday. It was great to be together with you and your teams, whether in person or virtually, as we welcomed Capital One Founder and CEO, Rich Fairbank, to Discover. This meeting was a significant event for both companies, and I hope you found it helpful to hear directly from Rich about Capital One’s heritage and culture, as well as his vision for our combined company.

As you heard, Rich spoke with great reverence about Discover and our history of award-winning customer service, the strength of our brand, our achievement in building a network, and the similarities we share in offering digital-first, tech-enabled products that meet the needs of tens of millions of customers every year.

As we continue our journey toward a successful merger, we understand your teams will continue to have questions, many of which unfortunately we will not be able to answer right away—not because we’re unwilling to share, but simply because we don’t yet have answers. I appreciate your understanding of this challenge and your recognition that this moment requires more of us as leaders of Discover.

During this time, it is our responsibility to not only acknowledge what our team members may be feeling, but also help them see the vision ahead for our new future. To that end, I’d like to highlight some key themes and important takeaways from our meeting with Capital One for you to reinforce with your teams:

 

  •  

Our Potential Opportunity

As industry leaders, Discover and Capital One have each been innovators who have sought to revolutionize banking with unique products and services. We excel in complementary areas, and Rich expressed confidence and excitement about the potential opportunities we hope to work toward as one combined company. Our merger, if approved, will enable us to deliver even more value to millions more customers.

 

Internal

  •  

Commitment to the Discover Brand

Respect and admiration for our brand is a significant part of what drew Rich and Capital One to Discover. They remain committed to the Discover brand living on in the network we built and as part of the Capital One banner of brands.

 

  •  

Commitment to Our Frontline Employees

Capital One has a deep appreciation for the customer service offered by both companies, is excited about the major geographies in which we operate today, and does not have any plans to lay off any of our front-line employees as a result of this deal.

 

  •  

Commitment to Chicagoland

Rich reaffirmed his commitment to Chicagoland—downtown, in Chatham, and in Riverwoods—as well as his compassion and respect for our employees.

As we pursue the merger, let’s continue to commit to serving our customers, strengthening our compliance and risk management framework, and Getting Better Every Day to help us meet our business goals.

I encourage you and your teams to remain up to date with our Merger Resources Site for updates during this period.

Thank you for your leadership, your commitment to our goals, and for all you do to support your teams and Discover. I know I can count on each of you to continue to look out for our customers and each other in the months ahead.

-Michael

Information about the directors and executive officers of Capital One and their ownership of Capital One common stock can also be found in Capital One’s definitive proxy statement in connection with its 2024 annual meeting of stockholders, as filed with the SEC on March 20, 2024, and other documents subsequently filed by Capital One with the SEC. Additional information regarding the interests of such participants are included in the joint proxy statement/prospectus and other relevant documents regarding the proposed transaction filed with the SEC when they become available.
