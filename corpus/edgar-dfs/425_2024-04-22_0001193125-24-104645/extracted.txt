The
following is a message sent to certain Discover Financial Services personnel by Dan Capozzi on April 22, 2024.

Since announcing our intent to merge with Capital One, we have expressed our commitment to keeping you informed, and this is the start of fulfilling that
promise.

Today, I am thrilled to share a positive development about Chatham, while also reinforcing the admiration Capital One has for the excellence we
have built in serving our customers. While this communication may not answer all your questions, I see this as the first of many steps toward solidifying a vision for a new, combined organization.

Chicagoland is the birthplace of Discover and Capital One recognizes the importance of our presence in this community and the investments we have made. To
that end, Capital One intends to fulfill the goal of bringing 1,000 jobs to the Chatham Customer Care Center and Shine Bright ® Community Center after the merger and to provide continued
support for nonprofit partners as they remain actively engaged in the community.

When we opened our Center in this vibrant Black community on
Chicago’s South Side, we set out to help people achieve brighter financial futures by providing jobs and having direct and long-term involvement with the city. Capital One’s commitment will ensure what we started continues into the future.

Our Chatham Customer Care center has contributed to our ability to exceed customer-satisfaction goals and perform at an industry-leading level as a
company. Like our more mature centers, its success is due to the strength of our employees locally in Chatham and to the contributions of their colleagues across the U.S. The unwavering support of South Side community leaders and organizations who
have welcomed us into Chatham and connected us with a talented pool of diverse candidates, suppliers, and contractors have also contributed greatly to our success.

Capital One’s commitment reflects our shared values of prioritizing our employees, customers, and the communities where we work and live. Furthermore, it
is an indication of their respect for our excellence in customer service, which is a testament to all of you.

While I know that this is welcome news to
us all, I also recognize it will raise more questions elsewhere. As other decisions are made, we will continue to share updates. I encourage you to continue to refer to the Merger Resources page for updates and answers to frequently asked questions.

Thank you for all you do every day to deliver a brighter future for our customers, our company, and our local communities.
