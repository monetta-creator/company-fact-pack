The following is a message sent by Michael Rhodes to Discover Financial Services employees on February 19, 2024.

 

A Message from Michael Rhodes

Today the Boards of Directors for both Discover and Capital One approved an agreement in which Discover will merge into Capital One. Pending regulatory approval, the combination of our organizations will bring together two mission-driven companies that are industry leaders with strong brands and a shared commitment to customer experience.

Discover has long been a disruptor in this industry, having pioneered no annual fee and 24/7 customer service, and introduced Cashback Bonus and Cashback Debit and free FICO scores. With this strategic decision, we will be part of another game-changing move in this industry. This merger will accelerate the progress we have made toward becoming the leading consumer bank and give us the ability to compete at a larger scale. It will also create strategic opportunities through the combined strength of Discover’s proven credit card business franchises and global payments network with Capital One’s digital-first consumer banking business.

I have admired Discover since its inception for a few key reasons. Our legacy as an industry pioneer. Our spirit of innovation and what we stand for in looking out for our customers. And the company’s mission to help people build brighter financial futures.

Clearly, Capital One shares my admiration. They respect what we do, they admire our brand and share our commitment to community. And they have indicated that they are committed to continuing the service excellence for which Discover is known. By joining forces, we will be better able to compete with the largest banks and payment companies in the U.S.

I understand this is unexpected and you will have many questions. Please keep in mind today is day one. There is still much work to do and decisions to be made about how we will combine our two organizations. Importantly, this requires regulatory approval, which may take some time. Until then, we remain two independent companies and there is no change in our mission, strategy, or the work we do every day to achieve it.

Nothing changes today. We must continue to take care of our customers and remain focused on our day-to-day roles and responsibilities, including our risk and compliance management.

The Executive Management Committee and I will host a Town Hall meeting today at 2:30 PM CT. In the meantime, attached please find an FAQ to help with initial questions.

This announcement may generate interest from outside our organization. If you are contacted by the media, please direct those inquiries to Matt Towson, Corporate Communications, at matthewtowson@discover.com.

Thank you for everything you do for Discover and our customers. Let’s work together to build upon Discover’s strong and successful history as we embark on this new chapter together.

Participants in Solicitation

Discover, Capital One and certain of their directors and executive officers may be deemed participants in the solicitation of proxies from the stockholders of each of Discover and Capital One in connection with the transaction. Information regarding the directors and executive officers of Discover and Capital One and other persons who may be deemed participants in the solicitation of the stockholders of Discover or of Capital One in connection with the transaction will be included in the joint proxy statement/prospectus related to the proposed transaction, which will be filed by Capital One with the SEC. Information about the directors and executive officers of Capital One and their ownership of Capital One common stock can also be found in Capital One’s definitive proxy statement in connection with its 2023 annual meeting of stockholders, as filed with the SEC on March 22, 2023, and other documents subsequently filed by Capital One with the SEC. Additional information regarding the interests of such participants will be included in the joint proxy statement/prospectus and other relevant documents regarding the proposed transaction filed with the SEC when they become available.
