Item 8.01 Other Events.

On August 30, 2017, Discover Bank (the “Bank”), a wholly owned subsidiary of Discover Financial Services, received notice from the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation that the June 13, 2014 consent order related to the Bank’s anti-money laundering and related compliance programs has been terminated. The termination was issued with no conditions.
