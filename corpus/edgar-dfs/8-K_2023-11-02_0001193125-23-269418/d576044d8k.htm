Item 8.01.

Other Events.

On October 30, 2022, the Company priced its offering of $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 7.964% Fixed-to-Floating Rate Senior Notes due 2034 (the “Notes”), at a public offering price of 100.000% of the aggregate principal amount, and entered into an underwriting agreement with Barclays Capital Inc., BofA Securities, Inc., Citigroup Global Markets Inc. and RBC Capital Markets, LLC, as representatives of the underwriters listed on Schedule 1 thereto (the “Underwriting Agreement”) relating to the Notes. The sale of the Notes closed on November 2, 2023. The Notes were issued pursuant to a Senior Indenture, dated as of June 12, 2007, between the Company and U.S. Bank Trust Company, N.A. (as successor in interest to U.S. Bank National Association), as trustee, as supplemented by the Second Supplemental Indenture, dated as of November 2, 2023 (the “Second Supplemental Indenture”). The offering of the Notes has been registered under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, by the Company pursuant to its Registration Statement on Form S-3 (Registration No. 333-257242). The Underwriting Agreement, the form of Note and the Second Supplemental Indenture are filed as Exhibit 1.1, Exhibit 4.1 and Exhibit 4.2, respectively, to this Current Report on Form 8-K and are incorporated herein by reference. In connection with the issuance of the Notes, Sidley Austin LLP provided the Company with the legal opinion attached to this Current Report on Form 8-K as Exhibit 5.1.
