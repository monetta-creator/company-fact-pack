Pursuant to the terms of the Indenture and the Notes, the Notes will be redeemed in full on March 15, 2021 (the “Redemption Date”), at a redemption price equal to the sum of 100% of the principal amount of the Notes redeemed plus accrued and unpaid interest to, but excluding, the Redemption Date.
