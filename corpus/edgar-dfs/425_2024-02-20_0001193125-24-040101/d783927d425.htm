The following communication was sent to certain international partners of Discover Financial Services on February 19, 2024.

 

We announced that Discover has agreed to merge into Capital One, which will be good for our Diner’s Club Franchises, Network Alliance Partners, and global Acquirers. You can read the press release we issued here.

This combination will bring together two industry leaders with strong brands and a shared commitment to the customer experience. Together with Capital One, we will be well-positioned to help consumers meet their financial goals by offering an expanded set of award-winning financial products.

Most importantly, we believe this agreement will help our global network better support your business. By coming together with Capital One, we will have new capabilities, significantly enhanced scale and scope, and more resources available to continue building a unique, diversified global payments platform. We expect to expand our network’s reach and relevance with consumers, driving more spending and increasing value for issuers, merchants, and cardholders alike. In short, this agreement is an investment in our ability to enhance the services that help you grow your business.

As far as next steps, the transaction is expected to close in late 2024 or early 2025, subject to satisfaction of customary closing conditions, including regulatory approvals and approval by the shareholders of each company. Until then, Discover and Capital One will continue to operate as independent companies. As we move forward, we expect this to be a seamless transition for all our stakeholders.

We greatly appreciate your business and the [longstanding] relationship that we have had with your company [over the years]. If you have any questions, please don’t hesitate to reach out to [me / your usual Discover contact]. We will keep you informed as we have updates to share.

Sincerely,

Participants in Solicitation

Discover, Capital One and certain of their directors and executive officers may be deemed participants in the solicitation of proxies from the stockholders of each of Discover and Capital One in connection with the transaction. Information regarding the directors and executive officers of Discover and Capital One and other persons who may be deemed participants in the solicitation of the stockholders of Discover or of Capital One in connection with the transaction will be included in the joint proxy statement/prospectus related to the proposed transaction, which will be filed by Capital One with the SEC. Information about the directors and executive officers of Discover and their ownership of Discover common stock can also be found in Discover’s definitive proxy statement in connection with its 2023 annual meeting of stockholders, as filed with the SEC on March 17, 2023, and other documents subsequently filed by Discover with the SEC. Information about the directors and executive officers of Capital One and their ownership of Capital One common stock can also be found in Capital One’s definitive proxy statement in connection with its 2023 annual meeting of stockholders, as filed with the SEC on March 22, 2023, and other documents subsequently filed by Capital One with the SEC. Additional information regarding the interests of such participants will be included in the joint proxy statement/prospectus and other relevant documents regarding the proposed transaction filed with the SEC when they become available.
