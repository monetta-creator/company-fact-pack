Variable Description 
Credit losses on investment securities (AFS/HTM)  
Projected credit losses on available- for-sale (AFS) and held-to-maturity (HTM) 
securities in billions of dollars for each quarter of the projection horizon.  
 
The Federal Reserve incorporates  its projection of expected credit losses on 
securities in the allo wance for credit losses, in accordance with ASU 2016-13 
Trading and counterparty losses 
Projected trading and counterparty losses in billions of dollars for each quarter of 
the projection horizon. 
 
Includes mark-to-market and credit valuation adjustment losses  and losses arising 
from the counterparty default scenario component applied to derivatives, securities 
lending, and repurchase agreement activities. 
 
Prior to the 2025 stress test , losses on private equity investments were included in 
trading and counterparty losses.  
 
Beginning in the 2025 stress test, losses on private equity investments  are not 
included in trading and counterparty losses . 
Trading losses 
Projected trading losses in billions of dollars for each quarter of the projection 
horizon. 
 
Includes trading profit and loss (P&L) and issuer default losses (IDL). Consistent 
with the Federal Reserve’s modeling principles, trading P&L is recognized in the 
first quarter of the projection horizon and IDL is recognized across the nine -quarter 
projection horizon. 
 
Prior to the 2025 stress test, losses on private equity investments were included in 
trading losses. 
 
Beginning in the 2025 stress test, losses on private equity investments  are not 
included in trading losses.  
Counterparty losses 
Projected counterparty losses in billions of dollars for each quarter of the projection 
horizon. 
 
Includes credit valuation adjustment and the largest counterparty default 
component. Consistent with the Federal Reserve’s modeling principles, 
counterparty losses are recognized in the first quarter of the projection horizon.  
Other losses/gains 
Projected other losses/gains in billions of dollars for each quarter of the projection 
horizon. 
 
Prior to the 2025 stress test,  included projected change in the fair -value of loans 
held for sale or held for investment and measured under the fair -value option, 
losses/gains on hedges on loans measure d at fair value or amortized cost. 
 
Beginning in the 2025 stress test, includes projected change in the fair -value of 
loans held for sale or held for investment and measured under the fair -value 
option, losses/gains on hedges on loans measured at fair value or amortized cost, 
and losses on private equity investments.  
 
Variable Description 
Losses on private equity investments 
Projected losses on private equity investments in billions of dollars for each quarter of 
the projection horizon. 
 
Prior to the 2025 stress test, private equity exposures were stressed using the global 
market shock component and were recognized in the first quarter of the projection 
horizon, consistent with the Federal Reserve’s modeling principles. 
 
Beginning in the 2025 stress test, private equity exposures are stressed using the 
macroeconomic scenario.
