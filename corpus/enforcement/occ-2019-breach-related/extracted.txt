TERMINATION ORDER 
 
WHEREAS, Scott Taylor and the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency stipulated 
to the issuance of the Consent Order containing a personal cease and desist order, dated April 3, 
1997, 97-19 (“Order”), pursuant to 12 U.S.C. § 1818(b) in connection with his activities at the 
New York Federal Branch of Caisse Nationale de Crédit Agricole (inactive). The Order 
prohibited him from engaging in any type of trading activity in any institution as defined by the 
Order, including insured depository institutions as defined by 12 U.S.C. § 1813(c).  
WHEREAS, the Comptroller of the Currency (“Comptroller”) believes the Order is no 
longer necessary to ensure the protection of customers, depositors, and shareholders of any 
institution or entity described by the Order. 
NOW, THEREFORE, the Comptroller terminates the Order.  
 
IT IS SO ORDERED this 27th day of August, 2020.
