Item 8.01 | Other Events

Issuance of
Class A(2017-3) Notes . On April 25, 2017, Discover Card Execution Note Trust issued $350,000,000 principal amount of DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-3) Notes pursuant to the Amended and Restated Indenture, dated as of December 22, 2015, as supplemented by the Second Amended and Restated Indenture Supplement, dated as of
December 22, 2015, each between Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer, and U.S. Bank National Association as Indenture Trustee, and the Class A(2017-3) Terms Document, dated as of
April 25, 2017, between Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer, and U.S. Bank National Association, as Indenture Trustee.

Use of Proceeds — Class A(2017-3) Notes . The public offering of
DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-3) Notes was made under the registration statement on Form SF-3 (File Nos. 333-205455, 333-205455-01, 333-205455-02) filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on
June 30, 2015 (as amended by pre-effective amendment no. 1 on September 10, 2015, pre-effective amendment no. 2 on November 20, 2015 and pre-effective amendment no. 3 on December 9, 2015 and declared effective on December 21, 2015) (collectively, the “ Registration Statement ”).

The public offering of the DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-3) Notes was terminated on April 25,
2017 upon the sale of all of the DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-3) Notes. The underwriters of the DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-3) Notes were Citigroup Global
Markets Inc., MUFG Securities Americas Inc., Barclays Capital Inc., Deutsche Bank Securities Inc., RBC Capital Markets, LLC, RBS Securities Inc. and SG Americas Securities, LLC. The price to the public of the DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-3) Notes was $350,000,000 (or 100.00000%).

During the period from the effective date
of the Registration Statement through the current reporting period, the amount of expenses incurred in connection with the issuance and distribution of the DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-3) Notes with
respect to underwriting commissions and discounts was

$787,500. After deducting the underwriting commission and discount described in the preceding sentence, the net offering proceeds of Discover Card Execution Note Trust before expenses for the
DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-3) Notes are $349,212,500. Other expenses, including legal fees and other costs and expenses, are reasonably estimated to be $540,000 and net proceeds of Discover Card
Execution Note Trust, after deduction of expenses, are reasonably estimated to be $348,672,500 total proceeds. With respect to the payment of these other expenses and costs, all direct or indirect payments were made to persons other than persons who
are (a) directors or officers of Discover Card Execution Note Trust or (b) owners of 10 percent or more of any class of securities of Discover Card Execution Note Trust.

The net proceeds of Discover Card Execution Note Trust, after deducting the underwriting commissions and discounts, and expenses above, were
paid to Discover Bank in exchange for an increase in the investor interest in receivables represented by the collateral certificate. Discover Bank will add those proceeds to its general funds. Except as provided in the previous sentence, none of the
proceeds were used for payments to (a) directors or officers of Discover Card Execution Note Trust or (b) owners of 10 percent or more of any class of securities of Discover Card Execution Note Trust.

Issuance of Class A(2017-4) Notes . On April 25, 2017, Discover Card
Execution Note Trust issued $400,000,000 principal amount of DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-4) Notes pursuant to the Amended and Restated Indenture, dated as of December 22, 2015, as supplemented by the
Second Amended and Restated Indenture Supplement, dated as of December 22, 2015, each between Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer, and U.S. Bank National Association as Indenture Trustee, and the Class A(2017-4) Terms Document, dated as of April 25, 2017, between Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer, and U.S. Bank National Association, as Indenture Trustee.

Use of Proceeds — Class A(2017-4) Notes . The public offering of
DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-4) Notes was made under the Registration Statement.

The public
offering of the DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-4) Notes was terminated on April 25, 2017 upon the sale of all of the DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-4) Notes.
The underwriters of the DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-4) Notes were Citigroup Global Markets Inc., MUFG Securities Americas Inc., Barclays Capital Inc., Deutsche Bank Securities Inc., RBC Capital Markets,
LLC, RBS Securities Inc. and SG Americas Securities, LLC. The price to the public of the DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-4) Notes was $399,807,480 (or 99.95187%).

During the period from the effective date of the Registration Statement through the current reporting period, the amount of expenses incurred
in connection with the issuance and distribution of the DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-4) Notes with respect to underwriting commissions and discounts was $1,400,000. After deducting the underwriting
commission and discount described in the preceding sentence, the net offering proceeds of Discover Card Execution Note Trust before expenses for the DiscoverSeries Class A(2017-4) Notes are $398,407,480.
Other expenses, including legal fees and other costs and expenses, are reasonably estimated to be $560,000 and net proceeds of Discover Card Execution Note Trust, after deduction of expenses, are reasonably estimated to be $397,847,480 total
proceeds. With respect to the payment of these other expenses and costs, all direct or indirect payments were made to persons other than persons who are (a) directors or officers of Discover Card Execution Note Trust or (b) owners of
10 percent or more of any class of securities of Discover Card Execution Note Trust.

The net proceeds of Discover Card Execution
Note Trust, after deducting the underwriting commissions and discounts, and expenses above, were paid to Discover Bank in exchange for an increase in the investor interest in receivables represented by the collateral certificate. Discover Bank will
add

those proceeds to its general funds. Except as provided in the previous sentence, none of the proceeds were used for payments to (a) directors or officers of Discover Card Execution Note
Trust or (b) owners of 10 percent or more of any class of securities of Discover Card Execution Note Trust.

Increase in
Principal Amount of Class D(2009-1) Notes . On April 25, 2017, Discover Bank, pursuant to Section 2.03 of the Amended and Restated Note Purchase Agreement (the “ Note
Purchase Agreement ”), dated as of January 1, 2016, among Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer, Discover Bank, as Sponsor, Discover Funding LLC, as Transferor, and the purchasers named therein and Section 2.06 of the Class D(2009-1) Terms Document, dated as of July 2, 2009, by and between Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer, and U.S. Bank National Association, as Indenture Trustee, increased the Outstanding
Dollar Principal Amount of the Class D(2009-1) Notes. The increase in the Outstanding Dollar Principal Amount of the Class D(2009-1) Notes was in the amount of
$69,672,132, resulting in an Outstanding Dollar Principal Amount of the Class D(2009-1) Notes of $1,787,786,887. The Class D(2009-1) Notes were sold to
purchasers in reliance upon the exemption contained in Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended. The Notes were sold at par value for cash, with no applicable underwriting discounts or commissions. In connection with this increase,
the investor interest in receivables represented by the collateral certificate issued by the Discover Card Master Trust I that secures the DiscoverSeries Notes was increased by $69,672,132. Discover Card Execution Note Trust paid the net proceeds
from the issuance to Discover Bank in exchange for the increase in the investor interest in receivables represented by the collateral certificate.

Extension of Expected Maturity Date and Legal Maturity Date of Class D(2009-1) Notes . On April 25, 2017, Discover Bank, as Calculation Agent on behalf of Discover Card Execution Note Trust, extended the expected maturity date of the Class D(2009-1) Notes to April 15,
2024 and extended the legal maturity date of the Class D(2009-1) Notes to October 15, 2026.
