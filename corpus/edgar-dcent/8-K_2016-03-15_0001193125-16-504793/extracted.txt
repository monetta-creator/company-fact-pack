Item 8.01 | Other Events

On March 20, 2010, Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer
(the “ Note Issuance Trust ”), and U.S. Bank National Association, as Indenture Trustee (the “ Indenture Trustee ”), entered into the Class A(2010-B) Terms Document (as amended, the “ Terms Document ”),
pursuant to which the Note Issuance Trust created a new tranche of Class A Notes of the DiscoverSeries (the “ Class A(2010-B) Notes ”). On March 15, 2016, the Note Issuance Trust, Discover Funding LLC, Discover Bank, U.S. Bank
National Association, the purchaser and committed purchaser of the Class A(2010-B) Notes and their agent entered into the Termination and Consent Agreement, pursuant to which the Note Issuance Trust paid the Class A(2010-B) Notes in full and the
Class A(2010-B) Notes were cancelled.
