Item 8.01.

Other Events

On March 29, 2012, Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer (the “Note Issuance Trust”), and U.S. Bank National Association, as Indenture Trustee (the “Indenture Trustee”), entered into the Class A(2012-A) Terms Document (as amended, the “Terms Document”), pursuant to which the Note Issuance Trust created a new tranche of Class A Notes of the DiscoverSeries (the “Class A(2012-A) Notes”). On April 30, 2021, the Note Issuance Trust, Discover Funding LLC, Discover Bank, U.S. Bank National Association, the purchaser and committed purchaser of the Class A(2012-A) Notes entered into the Termination and Consent Agreement, pursuant to which the Note Issuance Trust paid the Class A(2012-A) Notes in full and the Class A(2012-A) Notes were cancelled.
