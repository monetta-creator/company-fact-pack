Item 8.01. Other Events

On August 16, 2016, the commencement of the Accumulation Periods for certain tranches of DiscoverSeries Notes was delayed, pursuant to the terms of the Second Amended and Restated Indenture Supplement, dated as of December 22, 2015, by and between the Discover Card Execution Note Trust as Issuer and U.S. Bank National Association as Indenture Trustee, and the applicable terms documents thereto.

Class A(2011-4) DiscoverSeries Notes. The commencement of the Accumulation Period for the Class A(2011-4) DiscoverSeries Notes was delayed until the first day of the Due Period related to the November 2016 Distribution Date.

Class A(2014-2) DiscoverSeries Notes. The commencement of the Accumulation Period for the Class A(2014-2) DiscoverSeries Notes was delayed until the first day of the Due Period related to the February 2017 Distribution Date.

Class A(2014-3), Class A(2007-1), Class A(2014-5), Class A(2013-1) and Class A(2015-1) DiscoverSeries Notes. The commencement of the Accumulation Period for the Class A(2014-3), Class A(2007-1), Class A(2014-5), Class A(2013-1) and Class A(2015-1) DiscoverSeries Notes was delayed until the first day of the Due Period related to the April 2017 Distribution Date.

Class A(2012-4) DiscoverSeries Notes. The commencement of the Accumulation Period for the Class A(2012-4) DiscoverSeries Notes was delayed until the first day of the Due Period related to the May 2017 Distribution Date.

Class B(2014-2) and Class C(2014-2) DiscoverSeries Notes. The commencement of the Accumulation Period for the Class B(2014-2) and Class C(2014-2) DiscoverSeries Notes was delayed until the first day of the Due Period related to the June 2017 Distribution Date.

Class B(2014-3) and Class C(2014-3) DiscoverSeries Notes. The commencement of the Accumulation Period for the Class B(2014-3) and Class C(2014-3) DiscoverSeries Notes was delayed until the first day of the Due Period related to the July 2017 Distribution Date.
