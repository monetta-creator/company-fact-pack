Item 8.01.

Other Events

Class A(2010-C) Notes. On June 12, 2019, Discover Card Execution Note Trust (the “Note Issuance Trust”), as Issuer of the DiscoverSeries Class A(2010-C) Notes (the “2010-C Notes”), Discover Bank, as Master Servicer and Calculation Agent for the Note Issuance Trust, Discover Funding LLC, as Transferor, and the committed purchaser and purchaser of the 2010-C Notes and their agent agreed to (i) extend the existing termination date for the note purchase commitment to April 30, 2022, (ii) extend the existing liquidation commencement date of the 2010-C Notes to May 1, 2022, (iii) extend the existing expected maturity date of the 2010-C Notes to June 15, 2022, (iv) extend the existing expected principal payment date of the 2010-C Notes to June 15, 2022 and (v) extend the existing legal maturity date of the 2010-C Notes to December 16, 2024. In connection with any increase in the outstanding dollar principal amount of the 2010-C Notes, an interim expected maturity date and interim liquidation commencement date may be specified.

Class A(2010-D) Notes. On June 14, 2019, Discover Card Execution Note Trust (the “Note Issuance Trust”), as Issuer of the DiscoverSeries Class A(2010-D) Notes (the “2010-D Notes”), Discover Bank, as Master Servicer and Calculation Agent for the Note Issuance Trust, Discover Funding LLC, as Transferor, and the committed purchaser and purchaser of the 2010-D Notes and their agent agreed to (i) extend the existing termination date for the note purchase commitment to April 30, 2022, (ii) extend the existing liquidation commencement date of the 2010-D Notes to May 1, 2022, (iii) extend the existing expected maturity date of the 2010-D Notes to June 15, 2022, (iv) extend the existing expected principal payment date of the 2010-D Notes to June 15, 2022 and (v) extend the existing legal maturity date of the 2010-D Notes to December 16, 2024. In connection with any increase in the outstanding dollar principal amount of the 2010-D Notes, an interim expected maturity date and interim liquidation commencement date may be specified.
