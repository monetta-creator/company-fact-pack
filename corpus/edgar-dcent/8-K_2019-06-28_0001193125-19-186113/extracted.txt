Item 8.01. | Other Events

On December 21, 2010, Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer (the “ Note Issuance Trust ”), and U.S. Bank National
Association, as Indenture Trustee (the “ Indenture Trustee ”), entered into the Class A(2010-E) Terms Document (as amended, the “ Terms Document ”), pursuant to which the
Note Issuance Trust created a new tranche of Class A Notes of the DiscoverSeries (the “ Class A(2010-E) Notes ”). On June 27, 2019, the Note Issuance Trust, Discover
Funding LLC, Discover Bank, U.S. Bank National Association, the purchaser and committed purchaser of the Class A(2010-E) Notes and their agent entered into the Termination and Consent Agreement, pursuant
to which the Note Issuance Trust paid the Class A(2010-E) Notes in full and the Class A(2010-E) Notes were cancelled.
