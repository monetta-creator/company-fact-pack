Item 1.01 Entry into Material Definitive Agreements

On April 13, 2016, Discover Card Execution Note Trust, Discover Bank and Discover Funding LLC entered into an Underwriting Agreement by and among Discover Card Execution Note Trust, Discover Bank, Discover Funding LLC, Citigroup Global Markets Inc. and Barclays Capital Inc. (the “Class A(2016-2) Underwriting Agreement”), with respect to notes to be issued by Discover Card Execution Note Trust in one or more series, classes and tranches (the “Class A(2016-2) Notes”) pursuant to the Amended and Restated Indenture, dated as of December 22, 2015, as supplemented by the Second Amended and Restated Indenture Supplement, dated as of December 22, 2015 (as so supplemented and as otherwise modified or amended from time to time, the “Indenture”), and a Terms Document having the date stated in the applicable Terms Agreement, between Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer, and U.S. Bank National Association, as Indenture Trustee. A copy of the Class A(2016-2) Underwriting Agreement is filed as Exhibit 1.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.

On April 13, 2016, Discover Card Execution Note Trust, Discover Bank, and Discover Funding LLC entered into a Terms Agreement with Citigroup Global Markets Inc. and Barclays Capital Inc., with respect to the issuance of $550,000,000 principal amount of Class A(2016-2) DiscoverSeries Notes of Discover Card Execution Note Trust (the “Class A(2016-2) Terms Agreement”). A copy of the Class A(2016-2) Terms Agreement is filed as Exhibit 1.2 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.

On April 19, 2016, Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer, and U.S. Bank National Association, as Indenture Trustee, intend to enter into the Class A(2016-2) Terms Document. An unexecuted copy of the Class A(2016-2) Terms Document is filed as Exhibit 4.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.

In connection with the issuance of the Class A(2016-2) Notes, the chief executive officer of Discover Funding LLC has delivered a Depositor Certification for Shelf Offerings of Asset-Backed Securities, dated as of April 13, 2016 (the “Class A(2016-2) Certification”). A copy of the Class A(2016-2) Certification is filed as Exhibit 36.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.

On April 13, 2016, Discover Card Execution Note Trust, Discover Bank and Discover Funding LLC entered into an Underwriting Agreement by and among Discover Card Execution Note Trust, Discover Bank, Discover Funding LLC, Citigroup Global Markets Inc. and Barclays Capital Inc. (the “Class A(2016-3) Underwriting Agreement”), with respect to notes to be issued by Discover Card Execution Note Trust in one or more series, classes and tranches (the “Class A(2016-3) Notes”) pursuant to the Indenture and a Terms Document having the date stated in the applicable Terms Agreement. A copy of the Class A(2016-3) Underwriting Agreement is filed as Exhibit 1.3 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.

On April 13, 2016, Discover Card Execution Note Trust, Discover Bank, and Discover Funding LLC entered into a Terms Agreement with Citigroup Global Markets Inc. and Barclays Capital Inc., with respect to the issuance of $300,000,000 principal amount of Class A(2016-3) DiscoverSeries Notes of Discover Card Execution Note Trust (the “Class A(2016-3) Terms Agreement”). A copy of the Class A(2016-3) Terms Agreement is filed as Exhibit 1.4 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.

On April 19, 2016, Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer, and U.S. Bank National Association, as Indenture Trustee, intend to enter into the Class A(2016-3) Terms Document. An unexecuted copy of the Class A(2016-3) Terms Document is filed as Exhibit 4.2 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.

In connection with the issuance of the Class A(2016-3) Notes, the chief executive officer of Discover Funding LLC has delivered a Depositor Certification for Shelf Offerings of Asset-Backed Securities, dated as of April 13, 2016 (the “Class A(2016-3) Certification”). A copy of the Class A(2016-3) Certification is filed as Exhibit 36.2 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
