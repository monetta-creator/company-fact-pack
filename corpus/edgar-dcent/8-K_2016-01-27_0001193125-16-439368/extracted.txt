Item 8.01. | Other Events

In connection with the issuance of Class A(2016-1) DiscoverSeries Notes
(the “Notes”), Mayer Brown LLP, counsel to Discover Bank and Discover Card Execution Note Trust, has delivered (i) an opinion to Discover Funding LLC (as beneficiary of Discover Card Execution Note Trust), dated January 27, 2016,
regarding the legality of the Notes upon issuance and sale thereof on January 28, 2016; and (ii) an opinion to Discover Funding LLC (as beneficiary of Discover Card Execution Note Trust), dated January 27, 2016, as to certain federal
tax matters concerning the Notes. A copy of the opinion as to legality is filed as Exhibit 5.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K, and a copy of the opinion as to certain tax matters is filed as Exhibit 8.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K.
