Item 8.01.

Other Events

On September 18, 2018, the commencement of the Accumulation Periods for certain tranches of DiscoverSeries Notes was delayed, pursuant to the terms of the Second Amended and Restated Indenture Supplement, dated as of December 22, 2015, by and between the Discover Card Execution Note Trust, as Issuer, and U.S. Bank National Association, as Indenture Trustee, and the applicable terms documents thereto.

Class A(2014-1) and Class A(2016-1) DiscoverSeries Notes. The commencement of the Accumulation Period for the Class A(2014-1) and Class A(2016-1) DiscoverSeries Notes was delayed until the first day of the Due Period related to the January 2019 Distribution Date.

Class A(2016-2) DiscoverSeries Notes. The commencement of the Accumulation Period for the Class A(2016-2) DiscoverSeries Notes was delayed until the first day of the Due Period related to the March 2019 Distribution Date.

Class A(2014-4), Class A(2012-6), Class C(2016-1) and Class A(2016-4) DiscoverSeries Notes. The commencement of the Accumulation Period for the Class A(2014-4), Class A(2012-6), Class C(2016-1) and Class A(2016-4) DiscoverSeries Notes was delayed until the first day of the Due Period related to the May 2019 Distribution Date.
